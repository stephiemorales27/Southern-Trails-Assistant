# Knowledge base: Southern Trails Tours (fictional business)

Each line is a chunk loaded into the Simple Vector Store. The chunks are written in Spanish, which is what the assistant retrieves; an English translation follows for reference.

## Chunks (original, as loaded)

1. Horario de atención: lunes a sábado de 8:00 a 17:00 (hora de Nueva Zelanda). Domingo cerrado.
2. Punto de encuentro: 12 Harbour Street, Dunedin (frente a la oficina). Llegar 10 minutos antes.
3. Idiomas de los guías: inglés y español.
4. Tour Heritage Walk (2 horas): caminata por el centro histórico, con edificios patrimoniales y plazas. NZD 45 por persona. Salidas a las 10:00 y a las 14:00.
5. Tour Coastal Viewpoints (4 horas): recorrido en minibús por miradores de la costa y paisajes geográficos. NZD 95 por persona. Salida a las 9:00.
6. Tour Museum & Art Half-Day (4 horas): visita guiada a un museo y una galería de arte local. NZD 60 por persona, entradas incluidas. Salida a las 13:00.
7. Cancelación: gratis hasta 48 horas antes del tour. Después no hay reembolso.
8. Niños: menores de 12 años pagan la mitad en el Heritage Walk. En los otros tours pagan precio completo.
9. Otros destinos: además de los tres tours con salida desde Dunedin, Southern Trails Tours organiza tours por otros destinos de Nueva Zelanda y a las Islas Cook bajo consulta. Los precios, fechas y horarios de esos tours los confirma una persona del equipo.

## English translation (for reference)

1. Business hours: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 17:00 (New Zealand time). Closed on Sundays.
2. Meeting point: 12 Harbour Street, Dunedin (in front of the office). Arrive 10 minutes early.
3. Guide languages: English and Spanish.
4. Heritage Walk tour (2 hours): walk through the historic center, with heritage buildings and squares. NZD 45 per person. Departures at 10:00 and 14:00.
5. Coastal Viewpoints tour (4 hours): minibus trip to coastal viewpoints and geographic landscapes. NZD 95 per person. Departs at 9:00.
6. Museum & Art Half-Day tour (4 hours): guided visit to a museum and a local art gallery. NZD 60 per person, entrance fees included. Departs at 13:00.
7. Cancellation: free up to 48 hours before the tour. No refund after that.
8. Children: under 12 pay half price on the Heritage Walk. On the other tours they pay the full price.
9. Other destinations: besides the three tours departing from Dunedin, Southern Trails Tours organizes tours to other destinations in New Zealand and to the Cook Islands on request. Prices, dates and schedules for those tours are confirmed by a team member.
